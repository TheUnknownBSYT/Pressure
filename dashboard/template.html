How the model works

Pressure combines next-ball win-probability leverage (50%), urgency adjusted for contestability (30%) and recorded match stage (20%). High pressure starts at the 75th percentile from earlier quarters. Opponent-adjusted runs and wicket value are measured per six legal balls.

The approved score is the 20th percentile of 2,000 whole-match bootstrap estimates of pressure contribution. Success counts appearances above the player’s ordinary benchmark in comparable innings, phases and competitions. Matches get up to one vote, reaching full weight at six pressure balls.

Approved selection requires eight matches in each condition, 50 pressure balls, 100 ordinary balls, 75% matched coverage, positive pressure contribution and positive uplift. The default success threshold is strictly above 50%. Scenario controls rerank saved player results immediately. The underlying delivery labels and fitted models stay fixed.
